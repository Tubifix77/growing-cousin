# The "judge against the original need" rule ALONE made things worse

    ascii_plot.fixed (genuine)     5/5 ACCEPTED  ->  1/5   REGRESSION
    wake_catchup_fetcher (cosmetic) 0/5 green    ->  0/5   no change

Adding "work that answers your words precisely deserves more scrutiny" made the
judge suspicious of every repair without catching the fake one. Removed.

The testimony showed the real fault, and it was in the contract rather than the
model: "I can use these now. Next, I want it to fetch real news content." It SAW
the content was invented, filed that as a want, and accepted. The `want` field
was an escape hatch - any shortfall could be dressed as a future improvement.

Kept as the record of a fix that failed, and of the reading that found the
better one.
