# The want/claim invariant closed the cosmetic-repair hole

    wake_catchup_fetcher.fixed (cosmetic)  0/5 -> 5/5 RETURNED
    ascii_plot.fixed (genuine)             1/5           still failing

The rule: a `want` is for capability BEYOND the claim, never for the claim
itself. Before it, the judge saw the fabrication and filed it as a feature
request -- "Next, I want it to fetch real news content" -- then accepted. After
it, five times out of five: "the content and URLs are still placeholders /
simulated. I cannot use..."

It stopped judging by the date it had complained about and started judging by
whether the news was real. That is the difference between testing whether the
creature listened and testing whether the work is any good.

Remaining: the genuine repair was refused 4/5 over `bc: command not found` noise
on stderr while the chart below it was correct. Every rule in the brief was about
failure; none said what to do when the work SUCCEEDED messily.
